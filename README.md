# curious.pub

**Your agent builds. You approve. It ships.**

We're building the publishing platform for the agent era: a place
where your AI agent creates, deploys, and updates ultra-fast Astro
sites through MCP, and nothing goes live until a human says yes.

The web is about to get millions of new makers, people who describe
what they want and let their agents do the building. We think their
sites deserve craft, speed, and a quality seal, not a slop machine.
That's the whole idea, and we're having a great time building it.

## What lives here

- **cli** — the open-source `curious` command line tool and local MCP
  server. Inspectable end to end, MIT licensed, zero telemetry. What
  runs on your machine should never be a mystery.
- The control plane stays closed; the contract between us is open:
  the wire protocol ships in the CLI repo for anyone to read.
