# curious.pub

**Your agent builds. You approve. It ships.**

We're building a publishing platform for the agent era. Your AI agent can build, deploy, and update your site, but publishing remains a human decision. Nothing goes live until you say yes.

We think publishing is a human activity.

The web is about to welcome millions of new makers: People who describe what they want and let their agents do the building. Their sites deserve craft, speed, and care, not a slop machine. That's the idea behind Curious, and we're having a great time building it.

Today, Curious focuses on Astro sites, with more publishing experiences to come.

## What lives here

- **cli** — the open-source `curious` command-line tool and local MCP server. Inspectable end to end, MIT licensed, zero telemetry. Software running on your machine shouldn't be a mystery.
- The control plane stays closed. The contract between us stays open: the wire protocol ships in the CLI repository for anyone to inspect and build against.
